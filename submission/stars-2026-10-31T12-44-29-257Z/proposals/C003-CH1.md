# Conditional joint project: reproducing cold-store conveyor weld cracking

## Verified facts
- AutomVoj reports painted mild-steel hollow-section frames cracking at main crossmember-to-stringer welds in cold stores typically operating at 2–6 °C. [F1]
- AutomVoj reports cracking after about 18 months, while room-temperature loaded tests pass. It suspects interacting thermal cycling, condensation and loaded-belt vibration. [F2]
- AutomVoj has site photographs and crack measurements and retains two failed sections. [F3]
- AutomVoj lacks low-temperature cyclic-testing equipment and know-how. Erić’s chamber and fatigue machine are shared with two other groups. [F4]
- AutomVoj has no dedicated R&D department; its chief engineer and two senior automation engineers divide time between live projects and improvements. [F5]
- Erić’s thermal-fatigue study tested butt-welded S275JR plates between −30 °C and +20 °C. Out-of-phase loading produced 28% fewer cycles to initiation than the room-temperature baseline, illustrating potentially adverse interactions rather than an established remedy. [F6]
- Erić lists openness to joint projects involving low-temperature or combined-loading fatigue testing. [F7]

## Potential benefits
- For the company: The pilot could distinguish environmental interactions from fabrication or loading causes and identify whether a production change merits service validation.
- For the researcher: Subject to agreement, representative joints and service measurements could test whether established thermo-mechanical fatigue methods transfer to painted cold-store structures.

## Our inference
- This suggests a strong methodological fit, but much colder plate-joint experiments do not validate painted hollow-section connections at 2–6 °C, particularly under condensation.
- Fabrication defects, corrosion and local geometry could compete with thermal-fatigue explanations. Two retained sections likely cannot establish variability across all failures.
- The joint project remains conditional because researcher availability is unknown; openness to this format is not a scheduled commitment.

## Proposed next steps
- Propose a replicated eight-cell experiment on production-representative painted connections: thermal cycling off/on, condensation off/on, vibration off/on, covering baseline, singles, pairs and all three. Add AutomVoj’s room-temperature loaded comparator. Independently define levels: constant 4 °C versus measured cycling including 2–6 °C; controlled dry versus verified surface condensation; static belt load versus the same load plus measured vibration. Hold fabrication, coating and mean load comparable. AutomVoj would supply records, specimens and service measurements; Erić would lead fracture examination and testing. Measure cycles to detectable cracking, crack growth, location and fracture morphology. Proposed go/no-go: reproduce the observed connection failure and morphology, then select the best remedy on tuning specimens; on independently checked held-out specimens require at least 25% greater median cycles to cracking than unchanged joints under the reproduced service condition, without a new failure mode. Insufficient replication or mismatched damage means inconclusive, not pass; absent an interaction alone never closes the mechanism search.
- Before cutting either retained section, agree photographic documentation, nondestructive examination and destructive-sample allocation. Confirm steel, welding, filler, geometry and coating specifications; instrument service loads, temperature and moisture. Reserve shared facilities and verify humidity control, fixture capacity and safe combined operation; identify external facilities if needed.
- Negotiate two weeks for scoping and facility checks, followed by an eight-week pilot after booking. Agree replication and separate tuning/evaluation batches before testing. Assess manufacturability and coating compatibility for the best configuration, whichever it is. Accelerated results cannot establish 18-month reliability or elimination of warranty replacements; agree subsequent representative service validation before deployment.

## Open questions
- Can Erić confirm availability, and can both sides agree facility slots, budget, company engineering allocation and a decision deadline?
- Can AutomVoj supply specifications, representative new joints, failure histories and permission for selective examination of the two retained sections?
- Can customer-site access and laboratory controls support measured loading, independently controlled condensation and representative temperature cycles, including defrost or washdown?
