# Joint project: reliable Serbian support search across scripts and word forms

## Verified facts
- LinguaBot reports that about 18% of 12,000 real support queries from one bank client returned no useful result; its analysis attributed more than half of those misses to morphological mismatch or script confusion. [F1]
- The company describes an Elasticsearch baseline using a Croatian-built stemmer, within-word script mixing, and manual synonym maintenance that does not scale with quarterly terminology changes. [F2]
- LinguaBot reports around 85,000 labelled query-response pairs across all clients and two in-house linguists able to annotate or validate solutions. [F3]
- LinguaBot identifies approximately EUR 8,000 as its tooling/data budget. Its stated success criteria concern miss rate, maintenance and vocabulary updates. [F4]
- Dragić’s research maps tokens to phoneme sequences for script canonicalization. His morphological-reduction study investigates interactions between stemming, lemmatization and script normalization. [F5]
- Dragić reports two active projects and capacity for one smaller industrial contract or consultancy, with no interest in general software development lacking a research component. [F6]
- His neural-transliteration evidence reports a tradeoff: regular-token character error was higher for the transformer than deterministic rules, despite improvement on exceptions. [F7]

## Potential benefits
- For the company: The experiment could identify which components reduce actual support-search misses and whether terminology updates remain practical without rebuilding the index.
- For the researcher: Subject to agreement and authorized access, Dragić could test normalization interactions on industrial support queries and study transfer beyond legal and benchmark retrieval.

## Our inference
- This suggests a focused joint project on normalization interactions rather than general product development. Participation is conditional: joint-project willingness is open, but Dragić’s actual availability and allocation remain unconfirmed and limited in scope.
- Legal and benchmark evidence does not validate short banking or telecom queries, within-word typos, or vocabulary-rule changes without re-indexing. Incremental document insertion is not equivalent to updating rules against existing content.
- Normalization may address only part of the miss rate. Exception handling could introduce errors or maintenance burdens, so the best configuration may be simpler than a neural pipeline.

## Proposed next steps
- Propose eight configurations: existing Elasticsearch baseline; script canonicalization, Serbian morphological normalization and incremental vocabulary handling individually; every pair; and all three. Predefine independent on/off factor levels; hold corpus, ranking and queries constant. Split the 85,000 pairs into tuning and held-out evaluation samples; verify independence, preventing query, answer-template and client leakage. Select the best configuration on tuning data. Linguists would judge every evaluation occurrence at an agreed cutoff. Miss rate counts occurrences with no useful result divided by all evaluated occurrences, without deduplication; unresolved judgments count as misses. Proposed go/no-go: upper 95% confidence bound below 5% separately for the original bank-client population, initially and after updates; separate pre-agreed gates for telecom, within-word mixing and unseen terminology; no full re-index; agreed maintenance and latency caps under production load; prototype testable within six months and approximately EUR 8,000 tooling/data expenditure. Missing or insufficient evidence means no pass. A factor’s isolated null effect does not end interaction testing.
- Before tuning, agree separate miss-rate ceilings and upper-95%-bound acceptance rules for representative held-out telecom queries and real within-word-mixing/new-vocabulary strata, initially and after updates; pooled success cannot substitute. Keep constructed typo probes separate, with their own pre-agreed acceptance rule, not automatically the bank’s 5% target. Establish corpus access and usable judgments. Replay terminology updates against existing indexed documents, recording operations, retrieval changes, maintenance hours and latency. Unavailable evaluation data or unresolved acceptance rules block a pass.
- Propose month one for permissions, baseline reproduction and protocol agreement; months two through four for integration and tuning; months five through six for locked evaluation and update replay. LinguaBot would supply engineering and linguist validation; Dragić would guide canonicalization, morphological comparisons and error analysis, subject to his allocation. Test banking/telecom terminology, foreign names, abbreviations and alphanumeric identifiers explicitly. Validate Elasticsearch compatibility and update behavior rather than assuming prior implementations transfer.

## Open questions
