# Explaining chilled-service cracking in AutomVoj conveyor welds

## Verified facts
- AutomVoj reports cracking at main crossmember-to-stringer connections after about 18 months in cold stores typically operating at 2–6 °C. Its frames use painted mild-steel hollow sections; it reports replacing 14 complete frame assemblies under warranty this year. [F1]
- The company reports that room-temperature load tests pass. Its retained failure evidence consists of site photographs and measurements plus two failed sections in storage. [F2]
- AutomVoj reports lacking low-temperature cyclic-testing equipment and know-how. Erić’s notes state that his climate chamber and servo-hydraulic machine are shared with two other research groups. [F3]
- AutomVoj has no dedicated R&D department; its chief engineer and two senior automation engineers divide development effort between live projects and improvement work. [F4]
- Erić’s published work tested butt-welded S275JR plates under mechanical and thermal cycling. Out-of-phase thermal–mechanical loading produced shorter crack-initiation life than the room-temperature baseline. [F5]
- Erić lists joint projects involving low-temperature or combined-loading fatigue testing among his interests. [F6]

## Potential benefits
- For the company: The experiment could identify which environmental and loading combinations reproduce the actual failure, providing a defensible basis for choosing manufacturing changes rather than relying on room-temperature passes.
- For the researcher: Subject to agreement, Erić could test transfer of thermo-mechanical fatigue methods to painted conveyor connections, using production-representative specimens, retained fracture evidence and measured service histories.

## Our inference
- This suggests a relevant methodological match, but sub-zero plate experiments do not validate mechanisms or life predictions for painted hollow-section joints at 2–6 °C. Condensation, coating defects and manufacturing variability remain competing explanations; cold-induced embrittlement should not be assumed.
- Measured service histories and fracture comparison would likely distinguish meaningful reproduction from accelerated damage with a different cause. A factor showing no effect alone would narrow, not eliminate, interaction hypotheses.
- This joint-project proposal is conditional because Erić’s availability is unknown. Equipment access, condensation-control capability, customer-site access and company engineering time also remain unconfirmed.

## Proposed next steps
- Test production-equivalent painted joints with independently defined factors: stable chilled/measured thermal cycling, dry/controlled condensation, absent/measured belt vibration, retaining equal mean load. Include all eight combinations—baseline, singles, pairs, full combination—plus the room-temperature benchmark. Match geometry, welding and coating; randomise replicated specimens across cells and batches. Measure cycles to detectable crack, growth, strain, temperature, wetness, origin and morphology. Tune separately, verify independence, then evaluate held-out joints at 2–6 °C. Proposed go/no-go: the best configuration, whichever it is, must reproduce field location and morphology in at least two of three confirmation specimens under the frozen rubric. Prespecified contrasts on the primary field-matching-damage response must have simultaneous 95% confidence intervals accounting for specimen and batch scatter wholly beyond measurement uncertainty, with consistent confirmation direction. Unrelated strain or nonmatching-crack contrasts cannot qualify. Room-temperature reproduction is an alternative finding under identical gates, not proof of a chilled-specific mechanism. Missing data, unresolved references, inadequate precision or controls, and incompatible accelerated fractures mean inconclusive, not success. This tests mechanism transfer, not an 18-month service life.
- Before confirmation, freeze primary response (cycles to field-matching detectable crack), common exposure/stopping and censoring rules, explanatory contrasts, and blinded field-match rubric: location, origin/path, morphology and tolerances. Uncertain references block acceptance. Document both retained sections before allocating destructive samples. Propose AutomVoj supplies drawings, records, joints and site coordination; Erić leads examination, instrumentation and design. Monitor operational strain, vibration, temperature and humidity; verify wetness control. Separate pilots establish levels and detection limits; check independence across batches and service records.
- Negotiate twelve weeks after access and advance shared-equipment booking: four for evidence review and monitoring, two for pilots, six for factorial testing and confirmation, subject to achievable exposure and precision. Allocate company engineering time around live projects; agree costs, replication and confidence-interval procedures before starting. Subsequently compare feasible welding, filler or geometry changes under confirmed conditions before service trials; mechanism reproduction alone would not establish that warranty replacements will stop.

## Open questions
