# Reproducing and explaining chilled conveyor-frame weld cracking

## Verified facts
- AutomVoj describes painted mild-steel hollow-section frames cracking at crossmember-to-stringer welds after about 18 months of continuous use, with 14 assemblies replaced this year. [F1]
- The company reports cold-store operation at 2–6 °C and suspects combined thermal cycling, condensation and loaded-belt vibration; these are suspected, not established, causes. [F2]
- AutomVoj has site photographs, crack measurements and two retained failed sections. [F3]
- The company lacks low-temperature cyclic-testing capability. Erić’s chamber and servo-hydraulic machine are shared with two other groups. [F4]
- AutomVoj has no dedicated R&D department; its chief engineer and two senior automation engineers divide their time between live projects and improvement work. [F5]
- Erić’s published work compared room-temperature, cold-isothermal and combined thermo-mechanical fatigue programmes in butt-welded S275JR plates. [F6]
- Erić lists openness to joint projects involving low-temperature or combined-loading fatigue testing. [F7]

## Potential benefits
- For the company: The experiment could distinguish interacting service factors from assumed causes and identify whether its workshop test misses field-relevant damage, informing which production changes merit validation.
- For the researcher: Subject to agreement and access, Erić could test transfer of thermo-mechanical fatigue methods to painted conveyor joints using service measurements, failed sections and controlled comparisons.

## Our inference
- This suggests a relevant methodological match, but plate tests at substantially colder temperatures do not validate mechanisms in painted hollow-section conveyor joints at 2–6 °C.
- Condensation and coating effects remain a transfer gap; corrosion expertise may be needed. Two retained sections may not represent all replacements, and accelerated cracking could arise through a different mechanism.
- This joint-project proposal is conditional because Erić’s availability is unknown. His stated openness is not a project commitment, and shared equipment does not establish fixture compatibility or condensation-control capability.

## Proposed next steps
- Propose an initial experiment using newly made, painted production-representative crossmember-to-stringer joints and retained failures as references. AutomVoj would supply specifications, specimens and service-measurement support; Erić would lead inspection, fatigue testing and analysis. Compare the room-temperature workshop baseline with a chilled eight-condition matrix: baseline, thermal cycling alone, condensation alone, vibration alone, each pair and all three. Independently set thermal levels to constant temperature versus measured cycling, moisture to dry versus controlled condensation, and vibration to absent versus measured loaded-belt excitation; hold static loading, fabrication and other controls comparable. Include the actual 2–6 °C operating range and measured excursions. Measure cycles to detected cracking, crack location, growth and fracture features. Separate tuning specimens from held-out confirmation specimens and verify independence. Under the prelocked rules, proceed only if the best-supported configuration, whichever it is, reproducibly matches field failure and the contrasts support an explanatory hypothesis; insufficient evidence means no pass. A null single-factor effect does not exclude interactions.
- Before held-out confirmation, lock crack-detection thresholds, field-match features and tolerances, specimen-level replication, repeat-handling, runout and ambiguous-match rules, and the contrast decision rule. Propose at least three confirmation specimens per selected condition, all meeting the locked field-match criteria; count ambiguous matches as failures and runouts as non-reproductions. Predefine uncertainty-adjusted contrasts and justify loading and acceleration against approximately 18 months of service before interpreting causality. Unresolved criteria or unjustified exposure block confirmation.
- Propose two weeks for evidence-preservation planning, permitted sampling, service measurements and protocol agreement, followed by an eight-week pilot after advance laboratory booking; these are negotiation targets, not an endurance-validation promise. Confirm fixture, humidity and load compatibility and allocate company engineering time first. If reproduction succeeds, select feasible welding, filler or geometry changes on tuning data and negotiate independent validation criteria before production release.
