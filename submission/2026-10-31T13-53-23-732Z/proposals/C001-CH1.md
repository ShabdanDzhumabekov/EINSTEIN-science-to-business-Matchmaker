# Site-specific, explainable anomaly detection for ProtoSense

## Verified facts
- ArmaturaSec reports several terabytes of client captures collected over three years, but no confirmed malicious examples. Historical captures are not established as clean: the company notes that compromises could have gone unnoticed. [F1]
- ProtoSense passively inspects Modbus TCP and BACnet/IP traffic. ArmaturaSec reports that its previous supervised classifier produced enough false positives in deployments that clients began disabling alerts. [F2]
- ArmaturaSec describes site-specific normality and plain-language explanations as the focus of its task. [F3]
- The company reports hardware for on-device inference and a custom Yocto environment. Its hardware plan describes a dedicated security processor intended to support anomaly detection without affecting forwarding latency; the material does not establish its current availability. [F4]
- ArmaturaSec offers one to two part-time engineers and archive, bench and firmware access under NDA. Kiss's notes describe research-testbed access under a data-sharing agreement. [F5]
- Kiss's Modbus study constructed per-register baselines using request timing, payload values and function-code sequences. Her ICAROS project developed calibration procedures for detectors operating without labelled attack data. [F6]
- Kiss lists openness to joint projects with companies deploying ICS monitoring solutions. Her research also identifies a trade-off between false-alarm rate and response latency. [F7]

## Potential benefits
- For the company: The pilot could establish whether a site-specific detector produces manageable, understandable alerts on both protocols and identify appliance or BACnet gaps before deployment.
- For the researcher: Subject to agreement and permissions, Kiss could test transfer of unlabelled calibration methods to BACnet and heterogeneous client traffic, using archive replay and controlled scenarios to study drift, alert burden and explanation usefulness.

## Our inference
- Site-specific statistical baselines appear well matched to ProtoSense's lack of attack-labelled training data. This proposal remains conditional because Kiss's availability is unknown, despite her stated openness to joint projects.
- Modbus evidence suggests a useful starting point, not validation for BACnet, ArmaturaSec appliances or client operating modes. Contaminated archives and legitimate process changes could distort baselines; faster detection may also increase false alerts.

## Proposed next steps
- Propose a six-week pilot using site-specific chronological archive samples, treated as unlabelled/presumed normal, and isolated Modbus/BACnet bench scenarios. Separate training, tuning and held-out evaluation periods; check independence across repeated captures and operating cycles. Compare fixed-threshold rules with statistical/one-class baselines using timing, values and commands; test BACnet-specific features and operating modes explicitly. Train and calibrate without attack labels; select the configuration on tuning data, then freeze it. Evaluate held-out archives and separate injected scenarios for each protocol. Proposed go/no-go targets for the best selected configuration: at least 80% of injected episodes detected within 60 seconds, an upper confidence bound of at most one false-or-unresolved alert episode per site-day, and plant managers correctly answering at least four of five explanation-comprehension questions. Also require measured CPU/RAM/storage compliance on the actual Yocto appliance and no forwarding-latency increase detectable at an agreed bench resolution under matched throughput. Missing protocol evaluation, inadequate evidence or unavailable hardware means no pass, not inferred success.
- Propose counting alerts with the same site and reported cause within ten minutes as one episode; exposure is observed monitoring hours divided by 24. Review every alert episode, conservatively counting unresolved episodes as false. Randomly sample all evaluation windows, including non-alert windows, with recorded equal inclusion probabilities for expert review; do not infer missed-attack totals from these samples. Measure detection only on injected or confirmed cases. Estimate rate uncertainty using site-day blocks; insufficient exposure prevents acceptance.
- Subject to agreement, ArmaturaSec would supply permitted captures, operating logs, appliance measurements and isolated bench scenarios within its part-time capacity; Kiss would lead features, calibration and evaluation. Propose weeks one–two for permissions, budgets and splits, three–four for tuning, and five–six for locked evaluation and manager review. Establish NDA and data-sharing terms before access; do not assume the planned processor exists. Test legitimate drift separately and require operator approval before baseline resets.
